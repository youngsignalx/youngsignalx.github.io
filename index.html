ShortsBot

A personal automation tool for publishing YouTube Shorts.

ShortsBot is a private, single-user desktop application run by the owner of this site on their own computer. Once a day it takes popular Twitch clips from streamers who have given permission, edits them into vertical YouTube Shorts with captions, and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel as scheduled videos.

The app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to the owner's channel and to read public titles of popular Shorts as style examples. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from other people.
